# Product boundary

Research Skills Suite 1.0.0 contains four cross-disciplinary scientific workflows: evidence-traceable literature review, scientific writing and revision, reproducible computational research, and rigorous multi-artifact research coordination. It does not bundle domain genomics, workspace memory, office authoring, personal writing or resume methods.

The coordinator is an optional thin layer for lifecycle transitions and invalidation propagation, not a mandatory gateway for every scientific task. Other groups can be composed when their tasks are needed. No comparison establishes global uniqueness or universal quality; differentiation rests on inspectable contracts and measured checks. See MIGRATION.md for the pre-stable eight-Skill package.
