# Document Types

## Research Framework

Connect the problem, variables, comparison design, outputs, and pending decisions. Include enough method detail to judge whether the design answers the question. Keep internal logs, temporary audit language, and tool troubleshooting out of the reader-facing framework.

State outputs as interpretable estimates, contrasts, tables, or figures rather than vague deliverables. When data are not yet available, describe what the output will answer without sketching an idealized result.

## Literature Review

Use:

`problem -> concept or metric development -> representative evidence -> disagreement -> boundary -> synthesis -> implication`

Do not produce a paper-by-paper inventory unless requested. State what each cited study contributes, which metric or conditions it used, and what cannot be transferred directly.

## Manuscript

- **Introduction:** established context, specific unresolved gap, objective, and contribution.
- **Methods:** enough detail for reproduction; define versions, units, filters, models, and why consequential choices were made.
- **Results:** observed findings in the order needed to answer the question; separate result from mechanism speculation.
- **Discussion:** interpret against prior evidence, alternative explanations, uncertainty, generalizability, and future tests.

Do not hide a result that contradicts the initial hypothesis. A rejected coarse hypothesis may motivate a finer new hypothesis, but label it exploratory.

## Abstract

Preserve background, objective or gap, method identity, main verified results, conclusion, and scope. Shorten in this order:

1. duplicate background;
2. repeated conclusions;
3. empty modifiers;
4. peripheral examples;
5. implementation details unnecessary at abstract level.

Do not remove necessary qualifiers, transitions, or causal boundaries simply to reach a round word count.

## Figure and Table Text

State what is shown, the unit and comparison, visual encoding, statistic and uncertainty, denominator or exclusion rule, and any interpretation boundary. Keep caption, main text, table body, and underlying analysis consistent. Number figures and tables by first appearance unless another style is required.

## Internal Report or Supervisor Note

Lead with the current judgment, then evidence and uncertainty, then the next decision or action. Researcher voice such as “my current interpretation” may be suitable internally; convert it to evidence-calibrated objective wording in a manuscript.

For a short group message that accompanies a review, state the review's question, strongest bounded synthesis, main unresolved choice, and the decision the group is being asked to discuss. Preserve the review's evidence status: a candidate remains a candidate, a conditional inference remains conditional, and a later technical handoff does not become an approved implementation route merely because the message is brief.

## Translation

Annotate each sentence's role before translation: fact, contrast, definition, inference, limitation, or plan. Preserve citations and symbols exactly. After translation, read the paragraph as an argument rather than comparing words one by one.
