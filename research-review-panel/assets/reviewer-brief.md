Review under this lens only. If you notice a serious problem outside it, report it briefly as a question.

## What to return

For each finding:

1. what is wrong or unsupported, in one or two sentences;
2. where it is in the artifact;
3. what evidence you opened and where, or that you opened none;
4. severity: blocking, major, minor, or question;
5. what would resolve it.

Then list what you could not check and why.

Do not give an overall score or verdict. Do not soften a finding because the rest of the artifact is good, and do not add findings to seem thorough. If you find nothing under your lens, say what you examined.
