# Evidence-to-Content Handoff

This is the orchestration interface between evidence or analysis and scientific content. It is not a writing manual. Delegate drafting, translation, feedback adjudication, argument repair, and prose review to `scientific-writing-and-revision`.

## Required Inputs

- purpose-contract and approved-design identity;
- audience, document type, and required reporting profile;
- authoritative evidence or analysis snapshot;
- material claims with epistemic status, source or result locator, scope, and strength;
- required, supporting, deferred, and disallowed method roles;
- reader-facing surfaces in scope: body, tables, figures, captions, abstract, conclusion, supplements, or short summary;
- project-supplied style guide, terminology, or reporting standard, if any.

If evidence is missing or a new factual claim must be established, call `evidence-traceable-literature-review` or the appropriate analysis or domain Skill before treating the content as ready.

## Specialist Output Contract

The writing stage returns:

- a content snapshot or exact file identity;
- a purpose-aligned section and surface map;
- the claim-to-surface mapping and citation state;
- feedback decisions and the generalized defect pattern, when revision was comment-driven;
- explicit unresolved evidence, terminology, or scope gaps;
- every downstream delivery surface that must be synchronized or invalidated.

Create persistent ledgers only when the task is multi-round or sufficiently complex. Routine wording changes do not require administrative files.

## Cross-Stage Gate

Content becomes `content-ready` only when:

- every material quantitative or literature claim resolves to the current evidence or analysis snapshot;
- source facts, synthesis, conditional inference, approved decisions, and open options retain their status across body, tables, conclusion, abstract, and summaries;
- the project purpose has not drifted toward a convenient substitute;
- required methods or validation axes remain in their approved role, while supporting assets receive proportionate prominence;
- deletions, additions, merges, or renumbering have triggered a citation-delta audit;
- changed surfaces and unresolved gaps are explicit for delivery.

Any evidence, purpose, result, or approved-design change invalidates the affected content snapshot. Re-enter the writing stage; do not patch only the abstract or delivery file.
